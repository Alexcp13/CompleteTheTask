Design a premium mobile social fitness app called **LIFT KING**.

The concept is: **“Prove how strong you are.”**

Lift King is NOT a traditional workout tracker and NOT a professional powerlifting competition app. It is a global social network and competitive game for normal gym users who want to upload their strength achievements, compare themselves with other people around the world, climb rankings, unlock ranks, compete with friends, and show their progress.

The initial version should focus on **Bench Press**, but the entire visual system should be designed so that Squat and Deadlift can be added later.

The app should feel like a combination of:
- Instagram/TikTok for the social video feed
- Strava for rankings and competition
- Clash Royale / competitive games for ranks, progression and achievements
- A premium modern fitness app

Do NOT make it look like a generic bodybuilding app.

## BRAND

App name: **LIFT KING**

Logo: a minimal, powerful **“LK” monogram**, potentially combining the letters L and K into a simple crown or strength-inspired symbol.

The logo should be recognizable even without the text “Lift King”.

Visual identity:
- Dark premium interface
- Black / very dark charcoal background
- White typography
- One strong accent color, preferably electric gold/yellow or a similar premium color
- Subtle gradients
- Strong contrast
- Large numbers for strength statistics
- Modern, minimal cards
- Smooth rounded corners
- High-quality icons
- Subtle animations and gaming-style feedback

The app should feel premium, competitive and addictive rather than corporate.

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The entire application must be:

- Mobile-first
- Premium
- Dark
- Competitive
- Social
- Gamified
- Modern
- Highly visual
- Easy to understand
- Designed for short sessions and frequent returns

Avoid:
- Generic gym stock photos
- Excessive bodybuilding aesthetics
- Old-fashioned fitness UI
- Excessive text
- Spreadsheet-like rankings
- Corporate dashboards

Use:
- Large strength numbers
- Rank badges
- Profile cards
- Video thumbnails
- Progress bars
- Competitive statistics
- Subtle glowing effects
- Strong typography
- Smooth rounded cards
- Game-like feedback

The overall feeling should be:

**“This is where the world competes in strength.”**

Design the initial prototype with approximately 10–12 polished mobile screens and make the experience feel like a real startup product ready for launch.
